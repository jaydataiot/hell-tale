Feast of the First Lie

January 17 · Hell is known for having the most holidays to celebrate, and this one has earned its smoke.

🔥

What It Is

Celebrates the first lie ever successfully told. No one agrees who told it or what it was, and deliberately false histories of the holiday are part of the tradition.

What Visitors Need to Know
