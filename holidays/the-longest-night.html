The Longest Night

December 21 · Hell is known for having the most holidays to celebrate, and this one has earned its smoke.

What It Is

All artificial fires in Hell are extinguished for one hour. In the darkness, residents remember that Hell existed before many of its rulers, institutions, and explanations.
