The Feast of Innocents

December 28 · Hell is known for having the most holidays to celebrate, and this one has earned its smoke.

🔥

What It Is

Dedicated to people condemned despite not deserving their punishment. The Brazen Tribunal hears appeals throughout the day and the names of the wrongly judged are read aloud.
