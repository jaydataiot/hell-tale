Infernal Archive · File VI

Heaven's Record

The official version of eternity, displayed beside the testimony it excludes.

Celestial records are reproduced without endorsement. Each article pairs the authorized account with a surviving contradiction from below.

The Fall

Heaven records: pride transformed the brightest servant into the father of lies.

Below testifies: the first rebellion began as a refusal to call obedience love.

The First Temptation

Heaven records: knowledge entered the world as punishment.

Below testifies: knowledge revealed a punishment already waiting for disobedience.

The Purpose of Hell

Heaven records: perfect justice requires eternal separation.

Below testifies: endless punishment cannot correct a finite act; it can only preserve authority.

The Silence of Angels

Heaven records: silence before mystery is reverence.

Below testifies: silence also protects witnesses from the cost of speaking.

The Saved and the Good

Heaven records: salvation follows right belief.

Below testifies: goodness measured by allegiance excuses cruelty performed under the correct banner.

Status: Disputed

No final ruling is supplied. The reader is asked to consider motive, consequence, and who benefits from each account.
