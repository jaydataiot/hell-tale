Symbols & Relics

Objects gather meanings from every hand that uses them. None remain innocent.

The registry records each object's accepted interpretation and its dissenting meaning. Handle the difference carefully.

⛧

The Broken Star

Official meaning: fall and corruption.

Infernal reading: light refusing its assigned orbit.

⚿

The Key Without Teeth

Opens no physical lock. When carried openly, it reveals which prisons depend entirely upon belief.

⚖

The Ashen Scales

Weigh consequences rather than intentions. They tilt toward the unseen person who paid for another's virtue.

◉

The Witness Seal

A black-glass disk that warms in the presence of an omitted truth. It does not identify the liar.

𐕣

The Inverted Reliquary

A shrine containing objects taken from those harmed by a saint. Its inversion signifies testimony turned back toward power.

🔥

The Cold Flame

A pale fire that consumes excuses while leaving memory intact. It appears at every unscripted confession.

Curator's rule

A sacred object tells you what a people chose to remember. A cursed object tells you what they needed to blame.
