Infernal Archive · File II

Characters

Dossiers on the accused, the accusers, and every witness caught between Heaven and Hell.

These identities are assembled from contradictory testimony. Titles are not proof of virtue, and condemnation is not proof of guilt.

⛧

Satan · The Adversary

Rebel, prosecutor, tempter, scapegoat. He insists that opposition is not the same as evil and keeps every version Heaven discarded.

“If a question can destroy a throne, it was never truth that held the throne upright.”
⚖

The Ashen Clerk

An ancient functionary who records consequences without regard for creed. The Clerk never announces a sentence—only the weight of what was done.

♱

Father Caelum

A celebrated preacher certain of his salvation. His sermons comforted the powerful and sharpened shame into a weapon for everyone else.

✦

Mara Vale

A mortal contract-maker who enters Hell by choice. She suspects the bargain was an invitation to see the machinery behind obedience.

☿

Belial · Keeper of Terms

A demon of exact language. Belial cannot lie within a contract, but mortals rarely read the truth they are desperate to avoid.

☼

The Seraph Iophiel

Heaven's official witness. Radiant, composed, and increasingly troubled by omissions in the celestial record.

Unresolved identities

The archive contains references to The First Condemned, The Nameless Saint, and The Child at the Gate. Their files remain sealed pending testimony.
